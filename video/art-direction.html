FAST & SLOW

"Sometimes I Think Slow": art direction v2 with your notes applied (Champloo-inspired leads, in-world Karpathy)

One model, two modes, drawn as twin MCs in a late-night 1990s cel anime shot on VHS. Picture a jazz-noir TV anime from '98 taped off late-night TV: hand-painted backgrounds, flat cels, soft grain, a little chroma bleed, and small cyber details that give away that these two are models.

1. The look, and why

North star: the Mugen/Jin dynamic. A hip-hop anime about a wild improviser and a disciplined ronin who can't stand each other and win together. Its lo-fi beats DNA is exactly our lane. We borrow the dynamic and the energy, not the character designs: ours stay original.

Why cel anime and not camcorder realism.
  • It avoids uncanny AI faces and keeps both characters consistent across about 60 shots.
  • Late-90s jazz-noir anime is literally jazz: improvised FAST against composed SLOW.
  • It's a shared nostalgia for tech Twitter.
Picked from a three-model bake-off: see §6 for the alternates.
