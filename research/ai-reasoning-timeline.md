# AI "Thinking Slow" vs. "Thinking Fast": A Verified Timeline (2011 → 2026-09-28)

Research for **"Sometimes I Think Slow"**, an AI-POV homage to Nice & Smooth's "Sometimes I Rhyme Slow" (1991). The narrator is a model with two gears: fast single-pass answers (Kahneman's *System 1*) and slow deliberate reasoning / test-time compute (*System 2*). The audience is AI/tech/startup Twitter on X.

Compiled 2026-09-28. Sources are primary wherever possible: papers (arXiv v1 dates), lab posts, API docs, changelogs, system cards, and official X posts. Rows that rest on secondary coverage say so. **Era 5 (July to September 2026) comes after the lyric model's knowledge cutoff, so it got the most digging and is the most detailed.**

---

## How to read this

- **Date:** event date (arXiv v1, blog date, launch, or post), in US time unless marked UTC. `A → B` means announce → ship, or event → report.
- **Lane:** tags for the song's two personas and the forces between them:
  - `SLOW`: deliberate reasoning / test-time compute.
    - `SLOW (latent)`: reasoning in hidden states instead of words.
    - `SLOW (overthinking)`: the backlash against thinking too much.
  - `FAST`: instant/flash/haiku tiers, speed chips, diffusion, and speculative decoding.
  - `DIAL`: the switch between them (routers, effort levels, think buttons, toggles).
  - `WATCH`: chain-of-thought (CoT) monitoring, faithfulness, and interpretability.
  - `LONG`: long-running agents.
  - `FRAME`: the human origin of the System 1/2 idea.
  - `META`: markets, culture, and the industry.
- **Tiny detail (≤15 words):** a UI string, parameter, number, title, or short fragment you can drop into a bar. Quoted prose is kept to short fragments; the linked source has the full wording.
- **Why it lands:** a one-line pitch for the model's POV. These are pitches, not lyrics.

## Framing notes for the writer

- **The sample is already the joke.** "Sometimes I Rhyme Slow" is built on a sped-up loop of the guitar intro to Tracy Chapman's "Fast Car" (1988). It's a slow-rhyme song riding a fast-car sample, which is exactly the System 1 / System 2 tension. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sometimes_I_Rhyme_Slow · https://www.whosampled.com/sample/204/Nice-&-Smooth-Sometimes-I-Rhyme-Slow-Tracy-Chapman-Fast-Car/
- **The whole arc in five beats:**
  1. **2022–24:** humans *prompted* models to think ("Let's think step by step").
  2. **2024–25:** labs *trained* thinking in (o1, R1) and put a timer, a price, a budget, and a toggle on it.
  3. **2025:** everyone fought over routers, budgets, overthinking, and whether the visible thoughts were even honest.
  4. **2026:** the off-switch mostly disappeared. Flagships think by default ("always think; set the effort"), and speed moved into separate fast tiers, fast modes, and speed chips.
  5. **Sept 2026:**
     - GPT-6 Astra showed thinking can go *quiet*: shorter, less legible chains of thought and "neuralese" fears.
     - OpenAI's chief scientist revealed that the project that started reasoning models was literally called **"RLSlow"**.
     - Lab CEOs started saying the whole industry should slow down.
- **Motif to steal:** Anthropic's interpretability work caught Claude *planning the rhyme before writing the line*, in 2025 and twice more in 2026. A rap about thinking slow gets its own science.

## Corrections to the brief (premises that were wrong or imprecise)

**Precursors**
- **Noam Brown's "20 seconds ≈ 100,000×"** is the wording from his TEDAI San Francisco talk (2024-10-22), posted on TED.com on 2024-12-09. Earlier tellings differ:
  - Podcast, 2023-06-28: search on poker's final round was worth ~100,000×, with no time given.
  - UW lecture, 2024-05-23: about 30 seconds.
  - His July 2023 OpenAI-joining thread used "100,000×" for AlphaGo Zero's search, not poker.
  - His o1 launch thread has no poker stat.
- **"Pause/thinking tokens (2023)":** only Goyal et al.'s pause tokens are 2023 (Oct 3). "Thinking Tokens" (Herel & Mikolov) hit arXiv 2024-05-14, and "Let's Think Dot by Dot" is 2024-04-24.
- **Snell et al.:** the "beats a ~14× larger model" result is compute-matched and only holds where the small model already sometimes succeeds. On the hardest prompts, pretraining wins.
- **Strawberry meme:** there was no single viral moment. It was circulating by June 2024 and peaked in late August. Nothing confirms the "Strawberry" codename refers to it (Reuters reported the codename on 2024-07-12). o1 answered correctly at launch; stories of it failing are anecdotes about variant prompts.
- **Bengio's NeurIPS 2019 talk** was an invited talk, not a keynote. Claude's `<antThinking>` tag is known only from a leaked system prompt.

**o1 / o3 / R1 / s1 era**
- **`reasoning_effort` did not debut with o3-mini.** It shipped with o1 in the API on 2024-12-17. o3-mini (2025-01-31) brought it to a small model and to ChatGPT as "o3-mini-high".
- **DeepSeek-R1:**
  - Released 2025-01-20; arXiv v1 is dated 2025-01-22.
  - The App Store #1 was 2025-01-26 per Appfigures (widely reported on the 27th).
  - Nvidia fell 16.9% on Jan 27, losing $589B–$593B depending on the source.
  - Andreessen's "Sputnik moment" post was Jan 26.
- **o3's ARC cost per task** was restated several times, so cite a version. The famous "~$3,400/task" was a community estimate. FrontierMath 25.2% was OpenAI's internal number; Epoch measured ~10% for the released o3.
- **s1's "under $50"** is TechCrunch's framing. The paper reports 26 minutes on 16 H100s, and its s1K traces were distilled from Gemini 2.0 Flash Thinking.
- **HRM:** it claimed 40.3% on ARC-AGI-1, but ARC Prize verified 32%.
- **Grok 3's "Big Brain"** was announced but reportedly not live at launch.

**Anthropic / Claude**
- **The Claude Code keyword ladder** was first documented in CHANGELOG v0.2.44 (2025-03-15), not the April 18, 2025 best-practices post. The keywords were already in the day-one code (v0.2.9, 2025-02-24).
- **The code had three budget tiers, not four:**
  - `think` = 4,000 tokens.
  - `think hard` / `megathink` = 10,000.
  - `think harder` = `ultrathink` = 31,999.
- **The keywords faded in stages:**
  - 2025-09-29 (v2.0.0): all but `ultrathink` silently stopped mattering.
  - 2026-01-15 (v2.1.8): `ultrathink` became a no-op, and Anthropic deprecated it on 2026-01-16.
  - 2026-03-04 (v2.1.68): it returned as a one-turn high-effort nudge.
  - Today it's an in-context instruction only.
- **Tab toggled thinking only from 2025-09-29 to 2025-12-17**; since then it's Alt/Option+T.
- **Claude Plays Pokémon:**
  - The ~78 h in Mt. Moon was the *first* streamed run. The deliberate "blackout" strategy was the *second* run (~69 h).
  - The stream is researcher David Hershey's side project.
  - No 2025 model beat the game. Opus 4.7 did in May 2026, per community trackers.
- **Interpretability (Mar 2025):**
  - The model studied was Claude 3.5 Haiku.
  - The cosine example is two cases: bullshitting with no hint, and motivated reasoning with a hint.
- **Introspection (Oct 2025):** the term is "injected thought", not "intrusive thought". Detection was ~20% at best settings.
- **Claude 3.7 Sonnet's 128K thinking output** required a beta header; the default maximum was 64K.

**Other labs, 2025**
- **Qwen3:** the budget control mentioned in the Qwen3 blog lives in Alibaba Cloud's API. The open-weights template has only `enable_thinking` and `/think` / `/no_think`. Qwen announced it was dropping hybrid thinking on 2025-07-21 (Instruct-2507); Thinking-2507 followed 07-25.
- **Gemini 3 Pro's `thinking_level`** had only `low` and `high` at launch. `minimal` and `medium` arrived with Gemini 3 Flash (2025-12-17), and `minimal` doesn't guarantee zero thinking.
- **Apple's "Illusion of Thinking"** is arXiv v1 2025-06-07. The rebuttal's arXiv title is "Comment on…"; "The Illusion of the Illusion of Thinking" is the PDF title. v2 (06-16) removed "C. Opus" as an author.
- **The CoT-monitorability paper:** Hinton and Sutskever (and Bowman and Schulman) were *endorsers*, not authors.
- **IMO 2025:** OpenAI's result was graded by three ex-medalists, not certified by the IMO. Only DeepMind's gold was officially certified.
- **ChatGPT's skip button** was "Get a quick answer" at the GPT-5 launch and "Answer now" from GPT-5.1 on.
- **gpt-oss's harmony format** has three channels (`analysis`, `commentary`, `final`), not two.
- **`reasoning_effort: "none"`** arrived with the GPT-5.1 API (2025-11-13).
- **Wired's "router rolled back" story** (2025-12-16) covers Free and Go users only; paid tiers kept the router.

**Fast side**
- **Nvidia–Groq (2025-12-24)** was announced as a non-exclusive license plus hires. The ~$20B figure is CNBC's reporting; no price was disclosed.
- **OpenAI–Cerebras:** "$10B+" is CNBC's figure. OpenAI's post says 750 MW through 2028.
- **Groq's ~500 tok/s Mixtral figure** comes from demo posts (Feb 19–20, 2024). Groq's own release cites 241 tok/s on Llama 2 70B.
- **"GPT-5 Instant" wasn't a launch name.** The picker gained Auto / Fast / Thinking on 2025-08-12, and "Instant" came later.
- **Claude fast mode** launched 2026-02-07 at 6× the standard price ($30/$150 per million tokens). On Opus 5.5 it's ~2× ($8/$40). "Up to 2.5×" means output tokens per second.
- **There was never a Claude Haiku 5.** Haiku 4.5 (2025-10-15) was still the newest Haiku on 2026-09-28; Haiku 5.5 is only "coming weeks".

**2026**
- **No lab shipped latent reasoning as a product in H1 2026.** The H1 trend was *hiding* raw CoT (Anthropic's `display: "omitted"`, Meta's redaction). Reports that GPT-6 Astra uses recurrent depth came in September; OpenAI hasn't confirmed them and says its depth is within 2× of GPT-4.
- **GPT-6 exists.** Astra launched 2026-09-03; Sol and Luna followed 2026-09-22.
- **The ChatGPT router story ends in 2026:**
  - 2026-03-17: Auto was demoted to a checkbox.
  - 2026-06-10: the picker became an "Intelligence" ladder.
  - 2026-08-06: one GPT-5.6 Sol model serves both Instant and deeper reasoning, set by a slider.
- **The 2025 `/no_think` hybrid era gave way to "always think; set the effort."** In 2026 these models *can't* turn thinking off:
  - Claude Fable 5 / 5.1, Mythos 5 / 5.1, and Opus 5.5 (Opus 5 allows disabling only at `high` effort or below).
  - Kimi K3.
  - Qwen3.8 open weights.
  - Gemini 3.1 Pro.
  - Gemini 3.8 Flash dropped `minimal`.
