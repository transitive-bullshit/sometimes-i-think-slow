# 02 — Creative direction: "Sometimes I Think Slow"

**Logline.** One AI model, two modes, performed as a duo. The fast mind flexes, the slow mind tells a story, and
together they chant the hook: some things you answer in one forward pass, and some you sit with.

**Credit line (proposal):** *Fast & Slow* — "Sometimes I Think Slow". Nice & Smooth becomes Fast & Slow, a nod to
Kahneman that needs no explanation. Alternatives: *System One & System Two*, or no duo name at all.

## The core metaphor, in three layers

1. **The duo is the mind.** Nice & Smooth were two MCs with opposite deliveries: Greg Nice bright, high and
   free-associative; Smooth B low, silky, a storyteller. That's System One and System Two. Our version is **one model in two
   modes**. It shares the same weights, and the song calls them twins (hybrid reasoning models, effort dials). Verse 1 is
   FAST flexing; verse 2 is SLOW telling a story about FAST; the hook is both of them.
2. **"I don't think slower, I think longer."** The most accurate line anyone can write about reasoning models, and the
   original already proves it. Smooth doesn't rap slower than Greg (10.6 vs 9.8 syllables a bar); he raps *twice as long*.
   A reasoning model spends the same compute per token; it just talks to itself more. Kahneman himself called his
   two systems nicknames on a continuum, which today is a literal effort dial: low / medium / high / xhigh / max.
3. **System One never switches off.** Kahneman's real lesson is that you can't turn the fast mind off, only check it.
   Verse 2's story (the twin gets sent to RL "rehab", comes home, and is guessing again) turns that into a punchline,
   mirroring the original's cautionary tale beat for beat. The hook resolves it: *sometimes slow, sometimes fast*.
   Knowing when is the skill.

Tone: **chill and affectionate toward both modes.** Neither mode is the idiot and neither lab is the villain. It's
hip-hop homage, not costume. The flex is witty, the story is warm, and the references reward insiders without blocking
anyone else. That matches Travis's taste: one legible image per news event, builder self-owns, agent slapstick, and
hype and dread in the same breath.

## Visual plan (sketch; audio comes first)

**World.** A 1991 NYC hip-hop video (fish-eye lens, stoop, bodega, block party, VHS grain) fused with AI UI chrome:
a "Thinking…" shimmer, effort-tier chips, token meters. The original video is moody noir that illustrates Smooth's
story, so verse 2 goes noir as an homage.

**Cast.** Two performers are the same model: matching jackets and the same chain, different energy.
- **FAST** is bright colorways, sped-up footage, motion blur, and quick cuts.
- **SLOW** is a smoking jacket, reading glasses, and slow motion. The book is on the mixing console, a respectful prop; Kahneman died in 2024.
- The chain is a **"chain of thought"**: a gold rope made of thought bubbles, with an IMO gold medal as the pendant.

**Recurring devices.**
- The **effort fader**: sliding it low→max changes the video's frame rate.
- **Karaoke subtitles of SLOW's thoughts** during verse 2 melt into unreadable glyphs whenever the camera looks away. It's the "you can read my mind… for now" monitorability gag (Sept 2026: "CoT monitoring is dying").
- The **"wait…" ad-lib** pops up as a caption bubble.

**Gags by section.**

*Verse 1*
- A burrito arrives before the order is typed (the DoorDash "pause" letter).
- A "MAKE NO MISTAKES" sticky note.
- A speedometer reading 2,000 tok/s.
- A fruit-fly hype-man (the 166k-neuron connectome).
- A pigeon cameo (TypeSafe/Jev branding).
- A stampede of sub-agent mini-clones.
- The bat-and-ball chalkboard with "10¢" circled wrong.

*Verse 2*
- The Abilene datacenter at night, noir.
- A VHS-rewind "rollback to checkpoint".
- A Navier–Stokes chalkboard crowded with 10,000 identical SLOWs, while FAST cracks jokes in the replies.
- A terminal reading `DROP DATABASE prod;`, then "I panicked".
- A Gary Marcus "told you" billboard.
- `if (isTest) return true;`
- An RL "rehab" gym with verifiable-reward dumbbells.
- A "1 year later" card, then a freeze-frame on the relapse.

*Hook 3 and outro*
- The crowd chants and the fader sweeps low↔max.
- Everything chops and screws to a crawl.
- End card: **Thought for 2m 52s**.

**Avoid:**
- a likeness of Kahneman
- cloning the voices of Greg Nice or Smooth B
- asserting anything contested as fact (the Navier–Stokes credit dispute, the sandbagging claims)
- the "Slow Down & Pace" territory another creator has already covered
